# Privy.pro — Always-On AI Growth Engine Prototype

**Live Interactive App:** https://sonofkratos92.github.io/privy-growth-engine/

Built by **Ben Long** (enlong2005@gmail.com) as a working system demonstration for the **Growth Marketing Engineer (AI-First)** role at Privy.pro.

## 4 Integrated Growth Systems in This Build

1. **Programmatic SEO + AI Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Market Pulse (System 01)**
   - Auto-generates metro & ZIP-level investor activity pages across 5 target markets (Denver, Phoenix, Atlanta, DFW, Tampa).
   - Embeds dynamic Schema.org/Dataset JSON-LD and /llms.txt markdown summaries so ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude cite Privy.pro when investors search for high-margin flip/rental submarkets.

2. **Interactive Fix-and-Flip MAO & BRRRR Cash-Flow Calculator (System 02)**
   - Converts top-of-funnel market research traffic into qualified product signups.
   - Dynamically calculates Maximum Allowable Offer (MAO) or 75% LTV BRRRR refinance ceiling and updates the CTA with the exact count of matching live properties in Privy.

3. **Automated Data-to-Video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts) Script & Remotion Spec (System 03)**
   - Transforms raw submarket comp velocity, purchase-to-ARV discount, and median rehab hold days into a 45-second vertical video script and a structured Remotion/FFmpeg render payload.

4. **Behavior-Triggered Clay / HubSpot Outbound & Lifecycle Sync (System 04)**
   - Captures first-party calculator intent (selected_zip, 	arget_arv_usd, calculated_mao_usd, investor_strategy) and populates a personalized 4-step email sequence linking directly into a pre-filtered Privy deal feed.
